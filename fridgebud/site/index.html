FAQ

Questions

Is it only for dinner?

No. Send Bud a voice note about what you love and when you eat, and it plans breakfast, lunch or dinner around you.

What does it cost?

Nothing during early access.

Do I need an app?

Just Telegram, a free messaging app for iPhone and Android.

Will it message me a lot?

No. A couple of short messages a day at most, and only when food needs using. Text STOP any time.

Does Bud say if food is safe?

No. Bud plans what to use first; you check the food yourself.
