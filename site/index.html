House rules

Useful, then quiet.

No app

Bud lives in your messages. Nothing to install, nothing to keep tidy.

No nagging

One text a day at most. If nothing is at risk, Bud says nothing.

No guilt

Bud is dramatic about the food, never about you. No diets, no scores.

No safety calls

Bud helps you plan what to use first. Whether food is safe to eat is always your call.
